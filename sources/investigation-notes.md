# Threat Intelligence Investigation

## Investigation Objective
Investigate the FIN7 threat group using publicly available
threat intelligence sources, identify relevant indicators,
analyze adversary behavior, map observed TTPs to MITRE ATT&CK,
and produce actionable defensive intelligence.

## Threat Actor / Campaign

FIN7

MITRE ATT&CK ID: G0046

Associated Groups:
- GOLD NIAGARA
- ITG14
- Carbon Spider
- ELBRUS
- Sangria Tempest

## Initial Hypothesis

FIN7 is a financially motivated threat group whose operations
combine phishing, credential abuse, scripting, remote access,
command and control, data theft and ransomware-related activity.
